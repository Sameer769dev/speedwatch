Core Features

Everything You Need to
Understand Your Network

SpeedWatch goes beyond a basic speed test — it gives you real insight into what your internet connection is actually doing.

Live Speed Test

Animated speedometer tracks download and upload in real time. Results include ping, jitter, and a connection stability badge — all in one tap.

  • Download & Upload in Mbps
  • Latency (Ping) in ms
  • Jitter measurement
  • Rock Solid → Very Jittery stability grades

Historical Analytics

Every test is stored locally. View trends over time with beautiful charts, filter by date, and export data as CSV or PDF reports.

  • Timeline speed graphs (Vico charts)
  • Filter: Today / Week / All
  • CSV & PDF export
  • Unlimited history with Pro

Network Health Card

Detects Wi-Fi vs Cellular, identifies your band (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz), and validates internet connectivity at a glance — updated every time you open the app.

  • Wi-Fi & Cellular detection
  • Band identification (location-based)
  • Internet validation status
  • SSID & signal details

Background Monitoring

Set SpeedWatch to run automatic checks every few hours using WorkManager. Get notified if your speed drops unexpectedly — even when the app is closed.

  • Scheduled background speed checks
  • Push notifications on drops
  • Hourly checks with Pro
  • Data usage cap control (Pro)

ISP Plan Verification

Enter your contracted plan speed on first launch. SpeedWatch compares every test result against it and flags when you're not getting what you're paying for.

  • One-time plan setup
  • Visual "you got X% of plan" indicators
  • Persistent plan speed reference

PDF & CSV Reports

Export your test history as professional PDF reports or raw CSV data. Perfect for disputing ISP billing or proving speed issues to your provider.

  • Auto-generated PDF reports
  • Raw CSV export for spreadsheets
  • Share via any Android app
Technical Lab

Advanced Diagnostics
For Power Users

The Lab screen runs clinical network tests that expose hidden problems your ISP doesn't want you to find.

01

ISP Throttling Check

Reads the hardware-reported Wi-Fi link speed from your device chip and compares it to the actual measured throughput. A significant gap is a strong signal your ISP is throttling your connection.

Optimal Hardware Limited Suspicious Likely Throttled
02

Bufferbloat Analysis

Simulates heavy upload/download traffic and measures how much extra latency (the 90th percentile lag spike) is added under load. High bufferbloat causes lag in gaming and video calls even with fast internet.

A+ Excellent B Moderate D Poor
03

DNS Resolution Audit

Fires raw UDP packets to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Google (8.8.8.8) and compares response times against your ISP's default resolver. Recommends the fastest DNS server for faster web browsing.

Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 Google 8.8.8.8 System Default
Every lab test generates a signed Audit Certificate saved to your history — use it as evidence when disputing your ISP.
